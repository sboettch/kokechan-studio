Interactive Panel Forums · Oct 3 (Sat)

プレイグラウンド・対話セッション

Evolving past conventional academic lectures into dynamic neighborhood forums. Two curated conversational playgrounds that honor living craft, metabolic sociology, and grassroots urban resilience.

PLAYGROUND ONE 13:45–15:00 · Oct 3

長屋の生きた更新：工芸・芸術・自律的なまちづくり

The Living Nagaya: Craft, Art, and Autonomous Placemaking in Kyojima

Curated with Goto-san. How do century-old wooden nagaya and intimate alleyways flourish without erasing their intergenerational memories? Local Kyojima artists, woodworkers, and residents explore how shared alleys serve as communal living rooms and workshops.

Key Theme 1: Gentle integration of creative newcomers without resident displacement.
Key Theme 2: Micropublics: plant clusters, communal pumps, and daily mutual trust.
Curated by Goto-san & Kyojima Artisans Open Fishbowl Format
PLAYGROUND TWO 15:15–16:15 · Oct 3

メタボリック・ソシオロジー：適応する生態系としての生活空間

Metabolic Sociology: Living Communities as Adaptive Organisms

Drawing on ethnographic research (centered on researcher Yuka upon confirmation, or community spatial chroniclers). Re-interpreting Japanese Metabolism away from rigid megastructures toward the real shitamachi metabolism: organic timber repairs, seasonal adaptations, and social reciprocity.

Key Theme 1: Learning from the daily rhythms and survival networks of shitamachi elders.
Key Theme 2: Participatory digital archives that celebrate living dignity over museumification.
Featuring Yuka (Research) & Living Chroniclers Visual Dialogue
