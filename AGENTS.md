# Inclusive Languaging Protocol for All Agents & Future Projects

## Mandate & Core Principle
When generating copy, documentation, dialogue, UI text, metadata, tooltips, or public descriptions across all web apps and archives:
**AVOID AI INGROUP JARGON, EXCLUSIONARY SPEAK, AND TIMING-SPECIFIC EUPHEMISMS.**

Such terms alienate outgroup audiences (general readers, residents, commuters, shopkeepers, international visitors, and community members) and break trust. All copy must be **timeless, welcoming, respectful, and grounded in human-centered language**.

---

## Prohibited Terms & Required Replacements

| Prohibited Jargon / AI Euphemism | Approved Inclusive Replacement | Context & Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`spine` / `背骨`** | **`Main Corridor` / `Primary Railway Line` / `Central Promenade` / `主要幹線`** | Do not use anatomical/medicalized slang for roads, transit routes, or shopping streets. |
| **`this main IS the spine`** | **`this is the primary railway line connecting every station`** | Clear, direct, and welcoming to all readers. |
| **`pass`** (when used as AI jargon/euphemism) | **`Skip` / `Next` / `Review Step`** (Only use "Pass / 定期券" when referring to an actual railway transit ticket) | Prevents confusion and avoids AI developer jargon. |
| **`gate` / `檻` / `門`** | **`limit` / `time budget` / `commute boundary` / `上限` / `希望範囲`** | Avoid cynical or exclusionary metaphors ("cage / 檻", "gate / 門"). Use functional, empowering terms. |
| **`argues with the line`** | **`connects to the living corridor` / `relates to the transit network`** | Replace adversarial phrasing with relational, neighborhood-rooted language. |
| **`experiments passing through`** | **`neighbors, craftspeople, and ongoing community life`** | Center living residents and cultural practitioners rather than treating them as transient test subjects. |
| **`passage`** (abstract spatial jargon) | **`open lane` / `walkway` / `welcoming path`** | Direct, inviting spatial descriptions. |
| **`leverage`** | **`direct support` / `community investment` / `local stewardship`** | Replace corporate/VC buzzwords with grounded community action. |
| **`cadence`** | **`schedule` / `rhythm` / `routine`** | Everyday natural English. |
| **`synthetic`** (in public UI) | **`interpretive illustration` / `community reference model` / `visual study`** | Transparent without sounding artificial or disconnecting. |
| **`imputed` / `imputation`** | **`modeled` / `reconstructed` / `referenced`** | Avoid statistical/machine-learning jargon in user-facing contexts. |
| **`ground truth`** | **`field verified` / `on-site record` / `documented reality`** | Everyday research terminology. |

---

## Guidelines for Future Agents
1. **Audience Empathy**: Always write from the perspective of an everyday visitor, local shopkeeper, or neighborhood resident.
2. **Bilingual Elegance**: Ensure Japanese (`ja`) and English (`en`) copy carry equal warmth and dignity without robotic literalism.
3. **No Fluff / No Fillers**: Never use overused AI filler words like *delve, tapestry, testament, beacon, nexus, paradigm, bespoke, cadence*.
